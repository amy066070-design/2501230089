FUNDAMENTAL CONCEPTS

What is a Signal?

A signal is a mathematical function describing the variation of a physical quantity with time or space, used to convey information.

⚡

Electrical

Voltage & current in electronic circuits.

📡

Electromagnetic

Radio waves, Wi-Fi, and cellular transmissions.

💡

Optical

High-speed data through fiber-optic cables.

SIGNAL CLASSIFICATION

Analog vs. Digital Signals

01

Analog x(t)

Continuous in time and amplitude, susceptible to noise.

02

Digital x[n]

Discrete binary levels (0 and 1) for reliable transmission.

03

Periodic

Repeats patterns identically over period T.

04

Energy & Power

Classified based on total energy or average power.

COMMUNICATION SYSTEMS

Elements of a Communication System

🎙️

Source & Tx

Generates, processes, and modulates data for transmission.

🌐

Communication Channel

Physical medium subject to noise and signal attenuation.

🖥️

Receiver & Sink

Receives, demodulates, and recovers the original message.
